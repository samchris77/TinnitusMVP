Minimal redesign

Two functions, one main screen. ① 내 이명 찾기 — find your tinnitus pitch and loudness in three short steps. ② 소리 듣기 — play white, pink or brown noise or one of four nature sounds, with or without your tinnitus pitch notched out.

Try it

Everything in the phone works, and it plays real sound: tones and noise are generated in the browser, and the nature sounds are the app's own files.

Before you press play

  • Put on earphones and turn your computer volume down first. The finder plays pure tones, which can be sharp at high pitch.
  • The first screen is the one-time safety check. "아니요, 시작할게요" opens the finder.
  • Touching the pitch or loudness slider starts the tone, the way the app would.

Opened as a local file, the browser won't let the notch filter process the nature sounds (noise is fine). To hear the notch on nature sounds, serve the folder: python3 -m http.server 8766 in the TinnitusMVP folder, then open localhost:8766/docs/design/minimal-redesign.html.

What stays, what's new, what goes

Compared with the current four-tab build.

Stays

  • White, pink and brown noise — generated, no files
  • Four nature loops — rain, waves, brook, fire
  • Notch at the tinnitus pitch
  • Playback with the screen off, with a notification
  • Pitch matching with an octave check — rebuilt as steps
  • Colours and corner radii from Color.kt / Tokens.kt

New

  • Loudness matching — step 3
  • Octave check as a three-way choice — was a pop-up card
  • One main screen instead of 홈 + 소리
  • One-screen welcome with the red-flag check
  • Info sheet — disclaimer, privacy link, contact

Goes

  • Bottom tabs, 홈 ring and daily goal
  • 기록: calendar, history, day sheet, quick log
  • TFI questionnaire
  • Presets and the preset picker
  • Mixing noise and nature at once
  • 증폭 (amplify) mode
  • Sleep timer, mini player
  • Seven-page onboarding

Saved on the phone

  • matched_frequency_hz — existing key; unset until measured
  • has_tonal_tinnitus — existing key; false after "뚜렷한 음이 아니에요"
  • tinnitus_loudness (0–100), measured_at, sound_id, notch_on — new
  • onboarding_complete — existing key; now means the safety check was answered
  • Volume is the phone's media volume, so nothing to save
  • DataStore only. The Room database and its five tables go.

Audio engine work

  • Notch whichever sound is playing — today only the white-noise base is notched (audit F1)
  • About a one-octave band-stop centred on the pitch — today one biquad notch, Q 0.4
  • Real pink noise — today 핑크 is a one-pole low-pass (F2)
  • Probe mode for the finder: a pure tone or a hiss, nothing else — today the finder plays the therapy mix (B5)
  • Only the selected sound runs — today all four nature players run at once (F4)

Shape and space

  • Cards 16, buttons and sound tiles 12, chips 8, pills 100
  • 4-point grid: 16 at screen edges, 24 between groups, 8 between tiles
  • Every tap target at least 48 dp
  • Nothing smaller than 13 sp

Rules

  • Cards get a soft shadow and no border. Selected items get a 2 dp teal ring and no shadow.
  • One play button component, coral, at 72 / 64 / 44 dp.
  • Material Icons Filled only. No emoji, no text arrows.
  • Grey text is always Ink-2. Muted is 2.7 : 1 on Bg, too faint for text.
  • Korean everywhere, in 해요체.

Compose components

  • ScreenScaffold — app bar, content, footer
  • PrimaryButton, SecondaryButton, TextButton
  • PlayButton(size)
  • SoundTile, SwitchRow, ValueReadout
  • AppCard, ChoiceRow

Your calls

Each has a default, which is what the screens above show.

  1. Sleep timerCut. People who listen at bedtime may want sound to stop on its own. Bringing it back is one row on the main screen.
    Default: leave it out
  2. One sound at a timeNo mixing, for example pink noise under rain.
    Default: one at a time
  3. Safety check on the first screenThe only thing here you didn't ask for. It's in because docs/clinical/red-flags.md requires it and Play's health-app policy expects it. It costs one tap.
    Default: keep
  4. Notch on after measuringSaving a measurement turns 이명 음 빼기 on. People can turn it off.
    Default: on
  5. Ear choice in the finderTones play in both ears. Choosing left, right or both is cut.
    Default: both ears
  6. App name"이명 사운드" is a placeholder. The name and the application id must be final before the first upload.
    Default: none, you pick
