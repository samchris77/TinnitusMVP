Tokens

Each color's contrast is measured against what it sits on. Red figures miss the WCAG minimum: 4.5 : 1 for text, 3 : 1 for icons and large shapes. Calendar steps show the contrast with the step below; there is no pass mark for those, but bigger steps are easier to tell apart.

Calendar shades

Today's shades are teal at 14 % and 40 % opacity. The lightest is almost the colour of the card. The proposal uses solid colours with steps of similar size. In the dark theme, brighter means more listening.

One job per color

A colour that does one thing everywhere can be read without thinking.

Coral: play only

The one thing to press on the main screen.

  • The play button and its ring while playing
  • Never for links, chips or highlights — two coral things and play stops standing out

Red: red flags only

Reserved for "see a doctor".

  • The red-flag screen and its referral button
  • Never for a rising score, a missed check-in or a day without listening — those are information, not alarms

Teal: selected, on, progress

Everything else that needs colour.

  • Selected tiles, switches that are on, sliders, the primary button
  • The calendar shades and the score chart
  • Text stays in the two ink colours, never teal, except tappable words like 다시 찾기

Your calls

The defaults are what the screens show.

  1. How dark mode turns onFollowing the phone's dark mode needs no setting and matches what people expect. A switch in 앱 정보 could come later if anyone asks.
    Default: follow the phone
  2. Darker coral for the play buttonWhite on today's coral is 3.0 : 1, just at the minimum for a large icon. #C96F50 gives 3.6 : 1 and looks almost the same.
    Default: keep today's coral; the icon is large
  3. Dark calendar directionOn dark, brighter squares mean more listening, the way lit-up days read at night. The other choice, darker = more, would make long days disappear into the background.
    Default: brighter = more

What it takes to build

About half a day.

  • ui/theme/Theme.kt: a light and a dark Palette, chosen with isSystemInDarkTheme()
  • Two light values change: Muted and the calendar shades
  • enableEdgeToEdge picks light or dark status-bar icons to match
  • values-night/themes.xml for the splash background, so the app doesn't flash cream at night
  • Hard-coded Color.White on teal and coral becomes onTeal / onCoral

Dark colours are a proposal checked by calculation, not on a device. Screens vary a lot in the dark; check on a phone at low brightness before release.
