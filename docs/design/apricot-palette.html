Pastel apricot

Your reference palette applied to the app. The pastel fills stay as they are. Text and icons on them turn dark brown, because white on Soft Apricot is only 1.7 : 1, so the white button text in the reference can't be read by many people. Muted Terracotta handles the small things that must be read, and a warm night version is included.

Your calls

The defaults are what the screens show.

  1. Dark text on pastel buttonsThe play button and primary buttons are apricot with a dark brown icon or label. Keeping white would match the reference exactly but fails contrast badly (1.7 : 1).
    Default: dark brown on apricot
  2. Terracotta for small accentsLinks like 다시 찾기, selected outlines, the slider knob ring and the chart line use terracotta, so they stay readable. Apricot is kept for big fills.
    Default: as shown
  3. Red flags keep a real redTerracotta and the red-flag red are close in lightness, so the red-flag screen must also rely on its icon and wording, never colour alone. It already does.
    Default: #B23A2E, used only there
  4. Night versionApricot works better on dark than on white: 9.6 : 1 against the dark cards. It follows the phone's dark mode.
    Default: follow the phone
  5. App icon and splashShould move to apricot with a dark brown mark, so the app doesn't open teal and then turn orange.
    Default: update both

Contrast is calculated, not measured on a device. Pastel fills lose the most on dim screens and in sunlight; check on a phone before release. Pink noise's swatch (#EBB7B1) sits close to the new palette but stays distinct from the apricot selection outline, which is terracotta.
