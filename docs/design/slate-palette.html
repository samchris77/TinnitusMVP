Slate

Your reference, built dark first: near-black navy, deep slate, blue-grey and bone. There is no colour accent at all. Bone, the one light tone, marks what matters: the play button, the text, and the days you listened most. It fits an app people open in bed with the sleep timer. A light version on bone is derived from the same four tones.

What makes it modern, and what had to change

Monochrome works when lightness does the job colour usually does.

Lightness instead of colour

One light tone on dark is enough to lead the eye.

  • Bone play button with a dark icon: the brightest thing on screen
  • Selected tiles and the running timer use a lighter slate with a pale blue-grey outline, never a hue
  • The calendar runs dark → slate → bone, so the busiest days glow

Hairlines instead of shadows

Shadows vanish on dark backgrounds.

  • Cards are only a little lighter than the background (1.15 : 1), so each gets a faint 1 px outline
  • The player panel has a hairline on top instead of a shadow
  • The play button keeps a soft dark drop shadow so it lifts off the panel

Adjusted from the reference

Small changes so every role passes contrast.

  • Text is a brighter bone #E6DFD3 than the swatch (12.2 : 1 on the background)
  • Blue-grey #4F5B64 is too dark for text on dark, so links and outlines use a lighter blue-grey #A9BBCC (7.1 : 1)
  • Calendar step 1 is a step lighter than the cards so the quietest days still show
  • Red flags get a soft red #EC8C7E, used nowhere else

Contrast checks

The same twelve checks as the other palette pages. 4.5 : 1 for text, 3 : 1 for icons and large shapes.

Your calls

The defaults are what the screens show.

  1. Dark always, or follow the phoneMany sound and sleep apps are dark only, which makes the look consistent and suits bedtime. Following the phone shows the light version in daytime for people who use light mode.
    Default: follow the phone, with dark designed first
  2. No colour accentEverything is navy, slate and bone. The only colours on screen are the pink and brown noise swatches, which name the sounds. A single colour could be added later for one purpose, but nothing needs it now.
    Default: none
  3. Bone play buttonThe brightest element, with a dark icon (12.2 : 1). In the light version it flips to deep slate with a bone icon.
    Default: as shown
  4. Red flagsA soft red on dark, a clinic red on light, used only on the red-flag screen together with its icon and wording.
    Default: #EC8C7E / #B23A2E
  5. App icon and splashA bone mark on deep slate, so the app opens in the colours it runs in.
    Default: update both

Contrast is calculated, not measured on a device. Dark palettes vary a lot between OLED and LCD screens, and the hairlines and calendar step 1 are the first things to disappear on a dim screen; check on a phone before release.
