The palettes

Swatches, saturation compared with today, and the contrast checks. A check counts as passed at 4.5 : 1 for text and 3 : 1 for icons and large shapes.

Recommendation

안개 (Mist) feels the most open, and it has a matching night version.

Why 안개

The airiest of the three, without going cold.

  • Blue-grey reads as air and quiet, which suits a sound app
  • Clearly new, yet calm: accent saturation drops from 36 % to 24 %
  • Soft clay play is the only warm colour on screen, so it still draws the eye
  • The night version keeps the same hues for bedtime use with the sleep timer

When to pick the others

Both work and pass the same checks.

  • 세이지 (Sage) — keeps today's green family, so the app still looks like itself, only lighter. The safest change.
  • 라벤더 (Lavender) — the softest and most evening-like. It leans towards sleep and may feel less "everyday".

What makes it airy

Colour is only part of it.

  • Near-white tinted background (lightness 96–97 %, saturation 16–18 %)
  • Softer shadows, and hairline outlines on tiles so white on near-white still separates
  • A lighter scrim behind sheets
  • Ink stays dark: airy shouldn't mean grey, hard-to-read text

All contrast figures are calculated, not measured on a device. Low-saturation palettes lose more on cheap or dim screens; check on a phone at low brightness before release.
