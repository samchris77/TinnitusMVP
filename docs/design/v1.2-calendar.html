v1.2 design · 달력

A month calendar of listening time, at the top of 내 기록 (today's 내 변화 screen, renamed). It fills itself while sound plays, so there is nothing to enter. Darker days mean more listening. There is no daily goal, no streak and no daily rating; the weekly check-in stays the only score.

Try it

The phone starts on the main screen of someone four weeks in: listening on most evenings, three weekly check-ins, and this week's due. Today is Wednesday 21 October and nothing has played yet.

Things to try

  • Turn your computer volume down first, then press play.
  • Tap the calendar icon at the top right. Today's square fills in while sound plays. With the speed-up below, each second counts as 10 minutes.
  • Tap any day to see what was played and for how long. Try a blank day and a day with a dot.
  • Go back to September with ‹. It's the first month with a record, so ‹ stops there.
  • Do this week's check-in from the 내 이명 card. A dot appears on today.

Opened as a local file, the browser won't play the nature sounds through the notch. Serve the folder instead: python3 -m http.server 8766 in the TinnitusMVP folder, then open localhost:8766/docs/design/v1.2-calendar.html.

What it does

The calendar answers "how much have I been listening?" at a glance, and the day sheet answers "what did I play?". It deliberately doesn't judge.

1Where it lives

One screen for everything recorded, reachable from anywhere.

  • A calendar icon in the main app bar opens 내 기록
  • It works every week. In v1.1, 내 변화 was out of reach while a check-in was due.
  • Calendar on top; the v1.1 score, chart and history below, unchanged
  • The main screen otherwise stays as it is

2What a day shows

Recorded automatically while sound plays. Nothing to type or tap.

  • Shade by listening time: none, under 30 minutes, 30 minutes to 2 hours, 2 hours or more
  • A dot on check-in days, so the weekly score sits next to the listening around it
  • Today outlined. Future days dimmed and not tappable.
  • Tap a day: each sound played, notch on or off, and how long
  • Month line: days listened and total time

3What it leaves out

Each of these would make the calendar judge the person instead of record what happened.

  • No daily goal or ✓. TRT asks for about 8 hours a day with wearable devices (docs/clinical/sound-therapy.md). Most phone listening is evenings and bedtime, so nearly every day would read as a miss.
  • No streak. One bad night resets it.
  • No daily "how is it today?" That asks people to attend to their tinnitus every day, while sound therapy aims to draw attention away from it. The weekly K-TMI check-in stays the only rating.
  • No red or empty-day colour, and no editing.

All screens

Seven frames, including the states that matter: a day with nothing played, the first month, and a fresh install with no record at all.

What it takes to build

About a day. No database: one more value in the same DataStore file, and one timer in the playback controller.

Saved on the phone

  • listening — one entry per day, sound and notch setting, in seconds, e.g. 2026-10-18,rain,1,4200
  • About 1–3 entries a day, roughly 20 KB a year. Nothing leaves the phone.
  • The app doesn't record listening today, so the calendar starts empty on the day of the update.

Counting rules

  • Counts while a sound plays, from the main screen or 내 볼륨 찾기. The finder's test tones never count.
  • Saved every minute while playing and again on pause, so a killed app loses at most a minute
  • Sessions under a minute are dropped — accidental taps
  • A session counts toward the day it started. One started before 4 a.m. counts toward the day before, so a night of listening stays on one day.

Code

  • data/ListeningLog.kt: entries, encoding, the 4 a.m. rule and month totals, with unit tests for each
  • PlaybackController: notes the session's day on play, adds time each minute and on pause
  • MonthCalendar (7 columns, Sunday first) and DaySheet (same bottom sheet as the timer)
  • ProgressScreen becomes RecordsScreen; a calendar icon joins the main app bar
  • Each day has a spoken label, e.g. "10월 18일, 1시간 35분 들음, 체크인". Cells are about 42 dp, under the 48 dp guideline, as in most month grids; seven don't fit wider on a 360 dp phone.

Your calls

The defaults are what the screens show.

  1. Shade steps: 30 minutes and 2 hoursSet for phone listening, which is mostly evenings and bedtime. Steps near the 8-hour TRT figure would leave almost every day pale.
    Default: under 30분 · 30분–2시간 · 2시간 이상
  2. No goal, no streakA goal line or streak would raise use for some people, but it turns the calendar into a scorecard and most days into misses.
    Default: none; the month line counts days and hours
  3. When a day endsA session counts toward the day it started, with the day changing at 4 a.m. Splitting at midnight would spread one night over two squares.
    Default: start day, 4 a.m. boundary
  4. Rename 내 변화 to 내 기록, with an app-bar iconThe screen now holds listening as well as scores. The 내 이명 card row still opens it when the check-in isn't due.
    Default: rename and add the icon
  5. First day of the weekFollow the phone's locale, which is Sunday for Korean.
    Default: locale (일요일)
  6. Today's time on the main screenA line like "오늘 35분" under play would show the record without opening it, but it adds a line to the screen that should stay simplest.
    Default: not shown
